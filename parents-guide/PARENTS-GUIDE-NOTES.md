## Day 24: A Letter from Santa

Door 24 is extra special: it stays closed until Christmas Eve (December 24). Before then, your child will see a "Not quite yet!" message with a countdown of sleeps. Every other door can be opened at any time.

Behind Door 24, your child finds an envelope from the North Pole. Inside is a personal letter from Santa, written by you on Santa's own letter paper.

**How to write your child's letter from Santa**

1. Before Christmas Eve, open Santa's Letter Desk (grown-ups only):
   https://kimmiescolors-source.github.io/candy-cane-lane-advent/day-24/?grownups
2. Type your child's name and Santa's message. You might mention something they did this year that you're proud of, a kindness they showed, or a wish from their Letter to Santa (Day 2). The preview shows exactly how it will look, and tells you if the message is too long for the page.
3. Tap **Save Santa's letter on this device**.
4. Tap **Preview what my child will see** to check the envelope and letter.

**Important**
- Write the letter on the same phone, tablet or computer, and in the same browser, that your child will use to open Door 24. Don't use a private or incognito window.
- Writing on a different device? Tap **Copy a link for another device** and open that link once on your child's device. The letter will then be saved there.
- You can also save the letter as a picture (PNG) to keep, print or share.
- If no letter has been saved, your child will still find a warm letter from Santa on December 24.
- Door 24 uses the date on your child's device.
