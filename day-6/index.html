Prints at a true 7 × 5 inch postcard size on Letter or A4 paper: the front on page 1, the back on page 2. Set the scale to 100% (Actual size).

How to make it

  1. Print both sides. Card stock works best.
  2. Colour the sweet shop, Pip and all the treats.
  3. Cut out each side along the outer edge.
  4. Glue the two sides back to back, or print double-sided.
  5. Write your message, add the address and a stamp.
