How to make it

  1. Open your paper size above, then tap the purple button to make your own copy.
  2. Click “Your Name” and type your child's name. You can change the year too.
  3. Print it: Share, then Download as a PDF, and print at Actual size (100%).
  4. Colour both sides, then cut along the dashed lines.
  5. Glue the sides back to back, punch the ring hole and hang it with a ribbon!
